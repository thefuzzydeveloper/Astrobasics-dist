## 📥 Installation Guides

### 🪟 Windows Installation Guide (.exe)
1. **Download:** Click the [Windows Installer (.exe)](https://github.com/thefuzzydeveloper/Astrobasics-dist/releases/download/v-final-installer/astrobasics_installer.exe) link.
2. **Browser Download Warning:** In Edge or Chrome, if flagged as *"uncommonly downloaded"*, hover over the download item, click the three dots (`...`) ➔ select **Keep** ➔ tap **Keep anyway**.
3. **Bypass Windows SmartScreen:** When the blue *"Windows protected your PC"* window appears, click **More info** just below the header.
4. **Confirm Execution:** Click **Run anyway** at the bottom right corner.
5. **User Account Control (UAC):** Select **Yes** when prompted to complete the setup.

### 📱 Android Installation Guide (.apk)
1. **Download:** Click the [Android App (.apk)](https://github.com/thefuzzydeveloper/Astrobasics-dist/releases/download/v-final-installer/AstroBasics.apk) link.
2. **Open APK:** Swipe down your notification bar and tap `AstroBasics.apk`, or open your phone's Files / Downloads app and tap the package.
3. **Allow Unknown Sources:** If Android blocks installation:
   * Tap **Settings** in the prompt popup.
   * Toggle **ON** *"Allow from this source"*.
   * Tap the **Back** arrow to resume setup.
4. **Bypass Google Play Protect:** If Play Protect displays *"Blocked by Play Protect"*:
   * Tap **More details** (or dropdown arrow).
   * Tap **Install anyway** (do not tap OK).
5. **Launch:** Tap **Install**, then tap **Open** to run AstroBasics!
