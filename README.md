## Installation Guides

### Windows (.exe)

1. **Browser Download Warning:** In Edge or Chrome, if flagged as "uncommonly downloaded", hover over the download item, click `...` ➔ select **Keep** ➔ tap **Keep anyway**.
2. **Bypass Windows SmartScreen:** When the blue "Windows protected your PC" window appears, click **More info** located just below the header.
3. **Confirm Execution:** Click **Run anyway** at the bottom right corner to initiate setup.
4. **User Account Control (UAC):** When prompted, click **Yes** to complete installation.

### Android (.apk)

1. **Open Downloaded APK:** Tap `AstroBasics.apk` from notifications or your Files/Downloads app.
2. **Allow Unknown Sources:** If blocked, open **Settings** from the prompt popup, toggle ON **Allow from this source**, and go back.
3. **Bypass Play Protect:** If "Blocked by Play Protect" displays, tap **More details** ➔ tap **Install anyway**.
4. **Finish & Launch:** Tap **Install**, then **Open** to launch AstroBasics.
